On the Subject of Technicolored Squares

what

See Appendix SQUARE for identifying modules in Colored Squares family.

  • At the start, if there are not four colors that occur exactly twice each, you are looking at a different module.
  • The four colors that occur exactly twice are the rule colors. The leftover color is the odd color.
  • For each rule color, consult its table. Find the two cells whose positions correspond to the two squares of that color on the module, and apply the rules in those cells.
  • Within a rule, this square is the module square corresponding to that table cell; the other square is the other square of that rule color.
  • Each square earns points according to which of the applicable rules it satisfies.
  • Press all 16 squares in ascending order of points. If multiple squares have the same number of points, press those squares in reading order.
